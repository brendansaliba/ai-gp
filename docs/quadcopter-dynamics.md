## Important notation and concepts

We will define the position relative to some origin as a vector $P$ and acceleration defined as $P_dot$, which are given mathematiclaly as

$$ P = (x, y, z)^T $$ and $$ \dot{P} = (\dot{x}, \dot{y}, \dot{z})^T $$
